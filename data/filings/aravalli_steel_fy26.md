# Aravalli Steel Ltd — Annual Report FY2025-26 (fictional, for training only)

## Management discussion and analysis

Aravalli Steel produced 6.2 million tonnes of crude steel in FY2025-26, up 9 percent from 5.7 million tonnes a year earlier. Revenue from operations rose 11 percent to Rs 41,850 crore, helped by higher volumes of value-added flat products, which now make up 58 percent of the sales mix.

EBITDA was Rs 7,120 crore and the EBITDA margin was 17.0 percent, compared with 15.4 percent in FY2024-25. The improvement came from lower coking coal costs in the second half of the year and the commissioning of a waste-heat recovery plant at the Bhilwara works.

Net debt fell to Rs 9,300 crore from Rs 12,600 crore, and net debt to EBITDA improved to 1.3 times. The board recommended a final dividend of Rs 4 per share.

## Capital expenditure

The company plans capital expenditure of Rs 6,500 crore in FY2026-27, mainly for a 1.5 million tonne cold-rolling mill expansion due for completion in the fourth quarter of FY2027-28. The expansion will be funded through internal accruals and term loans.

## Key risks

The main risks are volatility in coking coal and iron ore prices, cheap imports if global steel demand weakens, and a possible delay in environmental clearance for the cold-rolling mill expansion. A 10 percent rise in coking coal prices would reduce EBITDA by about Rs 850 crore if it could not be passed on to customers.

## Shareholding

Promoters held 61.4 percent of the equity at year end, with no shares pledged. Foreign portfolio investors held 14.2 percent and domestic mutual funds 11.9 percent.
