# Konkan Power Ltd — Annual Report FY2025-26 (fictional, for training only)

## Operating highlights

Konkan Power operates 4,800 MW of generation capacity, of which 3,100 MW is coal-based and 1,700 MW is renewable (solar and wind). Renewable capacity grew by 600 MW during the year. Plant load factor at the thermal fleet averaged 71 percent against 68 percent in the previous year.

Revenue for FY2025-26 was Rs 18,240 crore, up 6 percent. Profit after tax declined 8 percent to Rs 1,510 crore because imported coal prices rose and one 500 MW unit was shut for 45 days for unplanned maintenance.

## Regulated returns and receivables

About 80 percent of capacity is tied up under long-term power purchase agreements with state distribution companies, which provide a regulated return on equity. Receivables from distribution companies stood at Rs 3,950 crore at year end, equal to 79 days of sales, down from 96 days after the payment security mechanism was strengthened.

## Strategy

The company targets 3,500 MW of renewable capacity by FY2029-30 and does not plan to add new coal capacity. It has signed a memorandum for a 250 MW pumped-storage project, which remains subject to state government approval.

## Key risks

Principal risks include delays in payments from state distribution companies, changes in tariff regulations, fuel supply disruption, and interest-rate risk on floating-rate borrowings of Rs 7,200 crore. A 100 basis point rise in interest rates would reduce annual profit before tax by about Rs 72 crore.

## Dividend

The board recommended a dividend of Rs 2.50 per share, a payout of 34 percent of profit after tax.
