# Deccan Pharma Ltd — Annual Report FY2025-26 (fictional, for training only)

## Business overview

Deccan Pharma makes generic formulations and active pharmaceutical ingredients. Revenue grew 14 percent to Rs 9,760 crore. The United States contributed 46 percent of revenue, India 31 percent and emerging markets the rest.

Research and development spending was Rs 780 crore, or 8.0 percent of revenue. The company filed 22 abbreviated new drug applications with the USFDA during the year and received 17 approvals, including a generic cardiac drug with limited competition.

## Profitability

EBITDA margin expanded to 24.5 percent from 22.1 percent due to a better US product mix and lower raw material costs. Net profit rose 21 percent to Rs 1,640 crore. The company is net cash positive, with cash and investments of Rs 2,100 crore and gross debt of Rs 450 crore.

## Regulatory matters

In March 2026 the USFDA issued a warning letter for the Visakhapatnam oral solids plant after an inspection found data-integrity lapses. The plant contributed about 9 percent of US revenue. Management has hired an external consultant to carry out remediation and expects to request a re-inspection within 12 months. New product approvals from this plant are on hold until the warning letter is resolved.

## Key risks

Key risks are price erosion in US generics, adverse regulatory action at manufacturing sites, currency movements (a 1 rupee change against the US dollar changes annual EBITDA by about Rs 45 crore), and dependence on China for some key starting materials.

## Outlook

Management guides for low double-digit revenue growth in FY2026-27, driven by new launches in the US and expansion of its chronic therapy portfolio in India.
