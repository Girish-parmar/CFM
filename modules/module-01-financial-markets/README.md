# Module 01 — Financial Markets, Instruments and Regulation

| Term | Weeks | Hours | Lab |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Market Foundations | 1–2 | 20 (4 live × 3 h + 4 clinics × 2 h) | [`lab01_markets_instruments.py`](../../labs/lab01_markets_instruments.py) |

## Learning outcomes

1. Describe the structure of Indian markets (NSE, BSE, MCX; SEBI and RBI; clearing corporations and depositories) and how they connect to global markets.
2. Explain each instrument class (equity, ETFs, government and corporate bonds, currency, commodity, futures, options) and who uses it and why.
3. Trace an order from placement to settlement: order types, matching, pre-open and closing auctions, circuit limits, clearing, T+1 settlement, margins.
4. Compute contract notional, margin and leverage, and futures fair value.
5. Adjust price histories for splits, bonuses and dividends.
6. List every cost and tax on a trade (brokerage, STT, exchange fees, SEBI fee, stamp duty, GST, capital-gains tax).

## Assignment

Write a 2-page note comparing the life of a retail equity delivery order with an index options order: who touches it, which margins and charges apply, and when cash and securities move. Use current exchange documents and cite them.

## Readings

- NSE and BSE websites: trading, clearing and settlement sections; current F&O contract specifications and lot sizes.
- NISM Series VIII workbook (Equity Derivatives): chapters 1–4.
- Larry Harris, *Trading and Exchanges*: chapters 1–5.
- M. Y. Khan, *Indian Financial System*: chapters on capital markets and regulation.
