# Assessment and Certification

## Components and weights

| Component | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Module quizzes (16 × online, 20 min) | 10% | Concepts from each module; lowest two scores dropped |
| Lab assignments (18 labs, graded through GitHub) | 25% | Correct, tested, readable code and written interpretation |
| Term examinations (2 × 4 h, proctored) | 20% | Week 9 (Term 1) and Week 26 (Terms 2–3): theory, numericals, short code-reading questions |
| Mini-projects (3) | 15% | End of Terms 2, 3 and 4: a strategy study, a risk/execution study and a tuned-ML or advanced-strategy study |
| Capstone | 30% | Research quality, code, paper-trading discipline, report and jury viva |

## Capstone rubric (30%)

| Criterion | Marks | Distinction-level evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hypothesis and pre-registration | 15 | Economic rationale written before testing; full list of trials declared |
| Data and engineering | 15 | Reproducible pipeline, point-in-time data, tests pass, clean repository |
| Backtest rigour | 20 | Realistic Indian costs, walk-forward, no look-ahead, Deflated Sharpe Ratio reported |
| Risk management | 15 | Sizing rules, drawdown limits, stress tests, RMS limits and kill switch configured |
| Paper-trading execution | 15 | 4 weeks run with a journal, reconciliation and honest comparison with the backtest |
| Report and viva | 20 | Clear write-up of limits and failure modes; confident answers to the jury |

Paper-trading P&L is **not** graded on profit. It is graded on discipline: following the pre-registered rules, respecting limits, keeping a journal and explaining deviations from the backtest.

## Grading bands

| Band | Overall score | Additional conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Distinction | 80% and above | Capstone ≥ 75% |
| Merit | 70–79% | Capstone ≥ 60% |
| Pass | 60–69% | Capstone ≥ 50% |
| Not yet competent | below 60% | Re-sit or resubmission allowed (see below) |

## Requirements for the certificate

1. Overall score of at least 60% and capstone score of at least 50%.
2. Attendance of at least 80% of live sessions (recordings do not count towards attendance) and both bootcamps.
3. All 18 labs submitted.
4. No upheld academic-integrity violation.

The certificate carries a unique credential ID that employers can verify online, together with a transcript listing modules, hours and grade band. It is a certificate of the institute. It is not a SEBI registration or a NISM certification; NISM exams are taken separately using the vouchers included in the fee.

## Re-sits and resubmissions

- One re-sit per term exam, within 4 weeks, capped at the pass mark (60%).
- Late lab submissions within 7 days lose 20% of the marks; after that they score zero but still count as submitted.
- A capstone that fails may be resubmitted once within 3 months for a Pass at most.
- A learner who misses a bootcamp for a documented emergency can attend the next cohort's bootcamp at no charge.

## Academic integrity and AI use

- Code and reports must be the learner's own. Discussing ideas is encouraged; copying code without attribution is not.
- AI assistants (including code assistants and LLMs) may be used for labs and the capstone if the learner discloses how they were used in a short note, and can explain every line in the viva.
- AI tools are not allowed in proctored term exams.
- Fabricated backtest results, undisclosed data snooping or misreported paper-trading results lead to failing the capstone.

## Feedback timelines

- Quizzes: instant.
- Labs: graded with written feedback within 7 days.
- Term exams and mini-projects: within 14 days.
- Capstone: jury feedback on Demo Day, final grade within 10 days.
