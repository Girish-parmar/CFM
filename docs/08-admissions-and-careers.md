# Admissions and Careers

## Eligibility

- A bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks, **or** final-year students who will graduate before Week 1.
- Mathematics up to Class XII (or equivalent). Engineering, commerce, economics, statistics, mathematics and computer science backgrounds are well suited; others are welcome if they pass the admission test.
- No programming experience needed; the pre-work covers it.
- A laptop with 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), a stable internet connection, and about 16 hours a week for 10 months.

## Selection process

| Step | What happens | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Online application | Education, experience, statement of purpose (300 words: why trading and why now) | Screening |
| 2. Admission test (75 min, online, proctored) | Quantitative aptitude, probability and statistics, logical reasoning, market awareness, reading a short Python snippet | 50% |
| 3. Interview (20 min) | Motivation, problem solving, a short case, and whether the time commitment is realistic | 40% |
| 4. Profile | Academic record, work experience, projects | 10% |

Offers are made on the combined score. Applicants who hold a CFA Level II+, FRM Part I+, CQF, or a postgraduate degree in a quantitative field may be exempted from the test (not the interview).

### Sample admission test questions

1. A fair coin is tossed 4 times. What is the probability of at least one head? *(15/16)*
2. A stock rises 10% on day 1 and falls 10% on day 2. What is the two-day return? *(−1%)*
3. Daily returns have a standard deviation of 1.2%. Assuming 252 trading days and independent returns, what is the approximate annualised volatility? *(≈19%)*
4. What does this print? `x = [3, 1, 2]; x.sort(); print(x[-1] * 2)` *(6)*
5. If the Nifty index is at 24,000 and the lot size is 75, what is the notional value of one futures lot? *(₹18,00,000)*
6. A strategy is tested with 100 different parameter sets and the best one is reported. Why might its backtested Sharpe ratio be misleading? *(Selection bias / multiple testing)*

### Interview rubric

| Criterion | 1 (weak) | 3 (adequate) | 5 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motivation | Wants quick trading profits | Interested in markets or quant careers | Clear goal, evidence of self-learning |
| Problem solving | Stuck on the case | Reaches an answer with hints | Structured, checks own work |
| Communication | Unclear | Clear | Clear and precise, explains uncertainty |
| Commitment | Unrealistic schedule | Plan for 16 h/week | Plan plus employer or family support |

## Career services

Career support runs throughout the program and for 12 months after graduation.

- **Career studio** (Weeks 36–38): CV and LinkedIn reviews, a GitHub portfolio that shows labs and the capstone, quant interview preparation (probability puzzles, statistics, coding, market questions), mock interviews with practitioners.
- **Employer connect**: hiring-partner sessions, Demo Day showcase, and a curated job board.
- **Alumni network**: an alumni community, quarterly meet-ups, and access to updated course content for two years.

**We do not guarantee placement, salaries or trading profits.** Career outcomes depend on the learner's performance, background, location and market conditions. Any placement statistics the institute publishes should be audited, show the base (number of learners who sought placement) and the reporting period, and must not be presented as a promise.
