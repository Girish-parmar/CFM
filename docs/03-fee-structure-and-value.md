# Fee Structure and Value

## Program fee

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Program fee | ₹4,25,000 |
| GST @ 18% | ₹76,500 |
| **Total payable** | **₹5,01,500** |
| Application fee (adjusted against the program fee on enrolment) | ₹2,500 |

GST at 18% is assumed because a private certificate course is a taxable service. If the certificate is awarded jointly with a university as a recognised qualification, the GST position may differ, so confirm it with your chartered accountant before publishing prices.

## Scholarships and fee waivers

One scholarship per learner (plus the early-application credit where noted). The institute caps total scholarships and discounts at 10% of the cohort's gross fee revenue.

| Scholarship | Benefit | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Merit | up to 20% (₹85,000) | Top 10% in the admission test and interview |
| Women in Finance | 15% (₹63,750) | Women applicants meeting admission standards |
| Armed forces | 15% (₹63,750) | Veterans and dependents of serving personnel |
| Need-based | up to 20% (₹85,000) | Household income below a published threshold; documents required |
| Early application | ₹20,000 credit | Complete application before the round-1 deadline; can be combined with one of the above |
| Alumni referral | ₹15,000 credit to referee and referrer | Referee enrols and completes Week 4 |

## Refund and deferral policy

| When the learner withdraws | Refund of fees paid (GST refunded proportionately) |
|---|---|
| 15 days or more before Week 1 | 100% minus the ₹25,000 seat-booking amount |
| Less than 15 days before Week 1, up to the end of Week 2 | 50% |
| After Week 2 | No refund. One deferral to the next cohort is allowed (₹10,000 admin fee) |
| Institute cancels or postpones the cohort by more than 30 days | 100% refund, or a free move to the next cohort |

The application fee is non-refundable. Refunds are paid within 30 days to the original payment method. Where an EMI partner is used, the refund goes to the partner first.

## Why ₹5 lakh is a fair price

- **Cost per guided hour.** ₹4,25,000 ÷ 460 guided hours ≈ **₹924 per hour** before GST (≈ ₹1,090 including GST) for small-group, practitioner-led teaching with lab support.
- **Depth.** It covers five areas that are normally sold separately: markets and derivatives, Python and databases, quantitative research and backtesting, risk and execution, and AI (ML, DL, NLP/RAG, automation).
- **Infrastructure most learners cannot buy alone.** Licensed exchange data, a static-IP trading server, GPU compute and a trading-floor simulation.
- **Evidence of skill, not only a certificate.** Every graduate leaves with a public GitHub portfolio, a pre-registered and reproducible research report, and a 4-week paper-trading track record reviewed by an industry jury.
