## Why this program exists

Algorithmic trading now makes up a large share of volumes on Indian exchanges, and the regulatory framework for retail algos introduced by SEBI in 2025 puts a premium on people who can build strategies that are tested, explainable, risk-controlled and compliant. Most courses teach either finance theory or coding, and rarely both to a professional standard. CFMAT joins the two:

1. **Markets first.** Instruments, market structure, derivatives, regulation and costs, the things that turn a backtest into real P&L.
2. **Engineering discipline.** Python, SQL databases, version control, testing, and an event-driven engine whose strategy code runs unchanged in paper trading.
3. **Research honesty.** Look-ahead bias, overfitting, purged cross-validation, walk-forward testing and the Deflated Sharpe Ratio are taught as core skills.
4. **Modern AI, used carefully.** Machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, NLP, LLMs and Retrieval-Augmented Generation for research, plus n8n for automation.
5. **Risk and compliance built in.** Pre-trade risk checks, kill switches, position sizing and the SEBI retail-algo framework are taught before anything goes live.

## Who it is for

- Graduates in engineering, commerce, economics, mathematics, statistics or computer science who want a career in quantitative finance.
- Working professionals in BFSI, IT or analytics moving into trading, risk or fintech roles.
- Discretionary traders who want to make their process systematic and testable.
- CA, CFA and FRM candidates or charterholders who want hands-on quant and programming skills.

No prior programming is required. The 40-hour pre-work and the entry assessment make sure everyone starts Week 1 with working Python and basic statistics.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the program a learner can:

1. Explain the structure of Indian and global markets, the life cycle of an order, and how equity, derivative, currency and commodity instruments are priced and settled.
2. Write clean, tested Python to acquire, clean, store and analyse market data in pandas and SQL databases.
3. Apply statistics and time-series methods (distributions, regression, stationarity, cointegration, bootstrap) to financial data.
4. Price options and futures, compute and interpret the Greeks, and build and hedge multi-leg option positions.
5. Design, implement and compare trend, mean-reversion, momentum, statistical-arbitrage and options strategies.
6. Backtest correctly: model Indian transaction costs, avoid look-ahead and survivorship bias, and use walk-forward testing and the Deflated Sharpe Ratio.
7. Measure and manage risk (VaR, expected shortfall, stress tests, position sizing) and build portfolios (mean–variance, risk parity, HRP).
8. Understand market microstructure and implement execution algorithms (TWAP, VWAP, POV, Almgren–Chriss).
9. Build trading infrastructure: broker API adapters, pre-trade risk management, kill switches, logging and paper trading, in line with SEBI's retail algo framework.
10. Apply machine learning, deep learning and reinforcement learning to trading without leaking future information.
11. Build NLP and RAG pipelines that extract information from news and filings and answer questions with citations.
12. Automate data pipelines, alerts and reports with n8n and AI agents.
13. Present an original, pre-registered, reproducible strategy research project to an industry jury.
