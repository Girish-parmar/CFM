# Mission, Vision and Values

## Mission

**To train people who can research, build, risk-manage and run systematic trading strategies
honestly — with the market knowledge, engineering discipline, statistical scepticism and
regulatory awareness that the Indian market now demands.**

We teach methods, not tips. A CFMAT graduate can take an idea from hypothesis to a pre-registered,
reproducible, cost-aware, risk-limited strategy running on paper, explain every number in it, and
say clearly when the evidence is not good enough.

## Vision

By 2030, CFMAT graduates are known across Indian trading desks, AMCs, brokers and fintechs for
three things: their research can be reproduced, their backtests survive live trading more often
than not, and they never confuse a lucky result with an edge.

## What we promise learners

| We promise | How we keep the promise |
|---|---|
| **Markets first.** You will understand the plumbing — orders, margins, settlement, costs, regulation — before you build on it. | Term 1 and M08–M09; Indian cost model in every backtest; SEBI's retail-algo framework in M17 |
| **Code you keep.** Every concept runs in a tested course library and 30+ labs you can reuse. | `cfmat` package with CI on every change; labs are plain Python you own |
| **Honest research.** You will learn to catch your own look-ahead, leakage, overfitting and multiple-testing mistakes. | Purged CV, walk-forward, deflated Sharpe, PBO and false-discovery control from M04 to M23 |
| **Risk before return.** Position sizing, limits and kill switches come before any live-like trading. | M13, M17, M18; paper trading only, with RMS limits |
| **Modern AI, used carefully.** ML, DL, RL, NLP, LLMs and RAG — with the evidence for and against each. | M19–M23 and M22's evaluation-first RAG |
| **A career asset, not only a certificate.** | Public GitHub portfolio, pre-registered capstone, industry jury, career studio |
| **Transparent fees.** One all-inclusive price, published in full with what it covers and what it does not. | [Fee plan](03-fee-plan.md), generated from the manifest |

## What we will never do

- Promise profits, a win rate, "passive income", placement or a salary.
- Give investment advice, tips, signals or stock recommendations — in class, in marketing or in alumni groups.
- Show P&L screenshots, lifestyle imagery or learner trading profits in marketing.
- Let learners trade real money inside the programme; the capstone is paper trading only.
- Hide a failing test, a planned-but-missing lab, or a negative result.

## Values

1. **Evidence over opinion.** A claim about markets is a hypothesis until tested properly, including ours.
2. **Reproducibility.** If it cannot be re-run from the repository, it did not happen.
3. **Candour.** Report negative results, declare every trial, say "I don't know".
4. **Risk discipline.** Survive first. Size so a bad month is survivable and a bad year is not fatal.
5. **Integrity.** Follow the letter and the spirit of market rules; never trade on privileged information or mislead others.
6. **Craft.** Readable code, tests, small functions, clear writing.
7. **Respect.** For learners of every background, for colleagues, and for the people on the other side of every trade.
