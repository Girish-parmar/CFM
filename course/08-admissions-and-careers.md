# Admissions and Careers

## Eligibility

- A bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% marks, **or** final-year students who will graduate before Week 1.
- Mathematics up to Class XII (or equivalent). Engineering, commerce, economics, statistics, mathematics and computer-science backgrounds are well suited; others are welcome if they pass the admission test.
- No programming experience needed; the four-week pre-work covers it.
- A laptop with 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), a stable internet connection, and about 18 hours a week for 12 months (12 guided + 6 self-study), plus three 3-day bootcamps.

## Selection process

| Step | What happens | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Online application | Education, experience, statement of purpose (300 words: why trading, why now, which [route](10-learning-routes.md)) | Screening |
| 2. Admission test (75 min, online, proctored) | Quantitative aptitude, probability and statistics, logical reasoning, market awareness, reading a short Python snippet | 50% |
| 3. Interview (20 min) | Motivation, problem solving, a short case, and whether the time commitment is realistic | 40% |
| 4. Profile | Academic record, work experience, projects | 10% |

Offers are made on the combined score. Applicants with CFA Level II+, FRM Part I+, CQF, or a
postgraduate degree in a quantitative field may be exempted from the test (not the interview).

### Sample admission test questions

1. A fair coin is tossed 4 times. What is the probability of at least one head? *(15/16)*
2. A stock rises 10% on day 1 and falls 10% on day 2. What is the two-day return? *(−1%)*
3. Daily returns have a standard deviation of 1.2%. With 252 trading days and independent returns, what is the approximate annualised volatility? *(≈ 19%)*
4. What does this print? `x = [3, 1, 2]; x.sort(); print(x[-1] * 2)` *(6)*
5. The Nifty is at 24,000 and the lot size is 75. What is the notional value of one futures lot? *(₹18,00,000)*
6. A strategy is tested with 100 parameter sets and the best one is reported. Why might its backtested Sharpe ratio mislead? *(Selection bias / multiple testing)*

### Interview rubric

| Criterion | 1 (weak) | 3 (adequate) | 5 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motivation | Wants quick trading profits | Interested in markets or quant careers | Clear goal, evidence of self-learning |
| Problem solving | Stuck on the case | Reaches an answer with hints | Structured, checks own work |
| Communication | Unclear | Clear | Clear and precise, explains uncertainty |
| Commitment | Unrealistic schedule | Plan for 18 h a week | Plan plus employer or family support |

## Career services

Career support runs throughout the programme and for 12 months after graduation.

- **Career studio** (Weeks 49–51): CV and LinkedIn reviews, a GitHub portfolio showing labs and the capstone, quant interview preparation (probability, statistics, coding, markets), mock interviews with practitioners.
- **Employer connect**: hiring-partner sessions (Week 50), the Demo Day showcase and a curated job board.
- **Alumni network**: an alumni community, quarterly meet-ups and two years of access to updated content.

**We do not guarantee placement, salaries or trading profits.** Outcomes depend on the learner's
performance, background, location and market conditions. Any placement statistics the institute
publishes are audited, show the base (learners who sought placement) and the period, and are never
presented as a promise.
